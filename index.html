Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the ReUse Market mobile app (package name: com.reusemarket.app) handles information. ReUse Market is a local marketplace app that lets you browse, save and list pre-loved items. We built it so that your information stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app does not connect to any server and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

  • Item listings that you create (title, price, description, city, name and phone number you type in).
  • Items you mark as saved.
  • A simple setting that remembers whether you finished the welcome screens.

All of this is kept in a local database on your phone. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Photos and permissions

If you add a photo to a listing, the app uses the system photo picker so you can select one image. The app only receives the image you choose and keeps it on your device. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or storage in the background.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or login, so no data is shared with third parties.

Your choices

You can delete any listing you created by swiping it away in the My Ads tab, remove saved items from the Profile tab, or clear everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be published on this page and the date above will change.
